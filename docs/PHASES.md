# Approval boundaries

Source: owner-supplied master brief, received 15 October 2026 (Philippine time).

1. **Foundation — current:** fresh project, Cloudflare-compatible build, private GitHub repository, tokens and typography, actual logo, responsive accessible shell, navigation, persistent theme/Lite/reduced-motion architecture.
2. Original 3D intro and hero, verified A1C vehicle, first-visit/replay behavior, scroll progression.
3. TDC then Online TDC experiences; both require review.
4. Motorcycle PDC, manual PDC, automatic PDC, reviewed individually.
5. Owner-selected student and facility media.
6. Verified Google reviews or free fallback, concise trust content, FAQ/enrollment information.
7. Read-only public-safe operations integration and fallback states.
8. Registration/Messenger/Facebook, verified map and final conversion sequence.
9. PWA, offline behavior, performance adaptation and mobile optimization.
10. Analytics, SEO, Open Graph, legal pages and 404.
11. BLOWBAGETS Coming Soon teaser only.
12. Final QA and **explicitly approved** production launch.

An owner question, silence or sending additional assets does not approve the next phase. Stop after reporting each phase's completed work, test results, limitations, and short review instructions.

## Content constraints

- Never fabricate reviews, facilities, vehicles, regulatory claims, prices or hours overrides.
- Preserve the supplied A1C logo artwork; other assets stay faithful to their references.
- No branch selector. Public content is Trece only.
- No floating Messenger CTA or aggressive opening CTA.
- No paid APIs/services without explicit approval.
- No public internal student/payment/instructor/vehicle-maintenance records.
- Existing operations app remains source of truth for approved promos and availability.
- Phone/address/map/vehicle transmission associations remain unset until verified.

## Public launch guard

Preview artifacts contain robots and header noindex directives. Do not remove them or deploy the production branch until Phase 12 approval. Production is deliberately absent from CI.
